VAL (Value Rating) Formula

ESPN Fantasy Standard

VAL is a composite efficiency metric that quantifies a player's total single-game impact for your Pick'em matchups. It rewards efficiency and defensive impact while penalizing missed shots and turnovers.

VAL = PTS + 3PM - FGA + (2 × FGM) - FTA + FTM + REB + (2 × AST) + (4 × STL) + (4 × BLK) - (2 × TO)
